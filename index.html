出町柳駅から徒歩2分。高野川の東岸、川端通沿いに建つ木造3階建ての新築住宅です。川の向こうには、京都屈指の邸宅街・下鴨が広がります。

A 2-minute walk from Demachiyanagi Station, this new three-storey wooden home stands on Kawabata-dori along the east bank of the Takano River. Across the water lies Shimogamo, one of Kyoto’s most distinguished residential districts.

A pitched roof with deep eaves and a planted front garden give the house a quiet, settled presence on the street. A sculptural spiral staircase links all three floors, and completion is scheduled for late November 2026.

二02 — ロケーション02 — LOCATION

ふたつの川が出会う場所Where two rivers meet

川kawa — river

ふたつの川が出会う場所Where tworivers meet

高野川の東岸、川端通沿い。下流へ少し歩けば、高野川と賀茂川が出会う鴨川デルタです。川の向こうには重要文化財・旧三井家下鴨別邸、その先に糺の森と世界遺産・下鴨神社。京都御苑、出町桝形商店街、京都大学も徒歩圏です。

The house stands on Kawabata-dori along the east bank of the Takano River. A short walk downstream is the Kamogawa Delta, where the Takano and Kamo rivers meet. Across the water stand the Former Mitsui Family Shimogamo Villa, an Important Cultural Property, and beyond it Tadasu no Mori and Shimogamo Shrine, a UNESCO World Heritage Site. The Kyoto Imperial Palace grounds, the Demachi Masugata arcade and Kyoto University are all within walking distance.

京の風景Scenes of Kyoto

春は対岸の桜、5月は葵祭、7月はみたらし祭。晩秋には糺の森が紅と金に染まります。出町柳から叡山電鉄に乗れば、鞍馬・貴船へも。

Cherry blossoms across the river in spring, the Aoi Matsuri in May, the Mitarashi Festival in July, and the forest turning gold and red in late autumn. From Demachiyanagi, the Eizan Railway runs north to Kurama and Kibune.

    the Forest of Tadasu

    下鴨神社の境内に広がる原生林。古代の山城の植生を今に伝えるといわれます。

    The primeval forest within the grounds of Shimogamo Shrine, said to preserve the vegetation of ancient Kyoto.

    the Semi Stream

    糺の森を流れる清らかな小川。古くから和歌に詠まれてきました。

    A clear stream that runs through Tadasu no Mori, celebrated in classical Japanese poetry.

    賀茂川と高野川の合流点。亀や千鳥のかたちをした飛び石で渡ることができます。

    The confluence of the Kamo and Takano rivers, crossed by stepping stones shaped like turtles and plovers.

    the Hollyhock Festival

    毎年5月15日、下鴨神社と上賀茂神社で行われる祭。京都三大祭のひとつ。

    Held every May 15 at Shimogamo and Kamigamo shrines; one of Kyoto's three great festivals.

    the Five Mountains Send-off Fires

    8月16日の夜、京都を囲む五つの山に「大」の字などの火が灯ります。

    On the night of August 16, fires in the shape of characters such as “大” light five mountains around Kyoto.

    Each July, visitors wade through the cool spring water of Shimogamo Shrine’s Mitarashi Pond to pray for good health.
